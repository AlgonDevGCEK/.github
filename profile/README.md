### 💡 About Us

**Algon Developer Community (ADC)** is the official multidisciplinary platform under **ALGON**, hosted at the **Government College of Engineering Kannur (GCEK)**.  
We bring together **business**, **media**, **finance**, **development**, **marketing**, and **coding** in a unified ecosystem that empowers students to:

- 💼 Work on **live projects**
- 💰 Earn **stipends**
- 🌍 Gain **real-world industry exposure**
- 🤝 Build **global professional networks**

ADC is **more than just a community** — it’s a launchpad for **innovation**, **collaboration**, and **career growth**.

---

### 🧭 Our Vision

> To create an ecosystem where students can collaborate across disciplines, gain hands-on experience, and grow into industry-ready professionals through real-world projects and mentorship.
